# Graybar document-channel procurement (local candidate, 16 October 2026)

Graybar is the confirmed distributor. This candidate uses retained supplier documents, not a live Graybar account connection. The authoritative Work Record owns Define, Develop, Design, Deploy and Operate decisions; `graybar_states` owns the linked procurement transaction. Supplier documents remain private, checksum-verified bytes. Graybar SKU and manufacturer part number are distinct; a stable product identity is separate from each changing quote offer.

The supported sequence is: approved Define and independently accepted Develop receipt → upload/review a versioned multiline Graybar quote → independent product/requirement assessment with retained specification → import eligible quote lines into a new Develop estimating revision → independent ROM review under the published pricing policy → reviewed Design package materials and approved BOM → PM purchase request → separate administrator PO approval → supplier-facing PDF export → controlled fictional test submission → retained acknowledgment → partial shipment → physical receipt → independently matched supplier invoice → Finance-posted commitment and reconciled incurred cost → serialized installed-equipment history on a supported Operate asset. Historical quotes, estimates, releases, receipts and Finance facts remain separate.

The quote-to-ROM bridge copies a reviewed line with its exact document, quote revision, price, pack basis, preliminary quantity and requirement reference into the existing Develop estimate. The estimate command's database guard rechecks those source fields; it does not change an approved estimate or customer price automatically. Design's approved BOM pins current Define, estimate and Design revisions. PO preparation checks BOM lines, offer validity, currency, account, unit, pack and budget. A nonzero stated freight charge is held until governed charge treatment exists; it is never silently omitted from the purchasing total. An explicitly zero-priced quote line needs a recorded reason. Unsupported taxes, rebates, credits and alternate currencies remain held.

The initial purchasing authority is an active workspace administrator, independent of the PM preparer. Operations or an administrator makes the technical BOM decision and records supplier/receiving facts. Finance records and reviews supplier invoices independently, then explicitly posts PO commitments and matched invoice costs to the existing fixed-price job ledger. Each incurred material line reconciles to one committed PO line, with unique source links; no supplier payment or customer billing is inferred. A purchasing approval never authorizes package release, crew publication or field start. If an approved Rybex-supplied BOM exists, the field-start guard requires sufficient physically accepted, unreturned material.

The supplier adapter advertises document intake capabilities and marks direct pricing/availability, RFQ, PO submission, acknowledgment, shipment and invoice channels unconfigured. Graybar describes e-commerce, punchout and electronic-business options [in its services overview](https://www.graybar.com/services), but no Rybex account entitlement, API/EDI protocol or enabled operation was supplied. Onboarding needs Graybar account owner, channel/protocol, credentials and environment, supported operations, supplier identifiers, idempotency/reconciliation contract, pricing and availability semantics, and test access. The `record-test-submission` command only retains a controlled local fixture event on a clearly fictional Work; it makes no network request to Graybar. An uncertain real submission must be reconciled with Graybar before retrying.

The server-side AI assistant interface is present but explicitly unavailable because no usable provider is configured. PDF text and CSV/XLSX cells are extracted deterministically and labeled for human review; these are not verified live AI. Recommendations cannot write decisions. Supplier text is treated as untrusted source content. OCR/scanned PDFs, advanced product comparisons and automated matching are not qualified.

No automation rule grants an external commitment. Any later rule must pin approved supplier/account and BOM, maximum amount and quantity, price tolerance, required delivery and substitution limits; an unconfigured rule means manual independent approval. Installed serials are linked only to supported assets after physical receipt. Consumables are not registered as fleet equipment.

## Work economic authority for Graybar POs (local, 17 October 2026)

A PM-entered PO budget no longer grants purchasing permission. On new Work, an independent administrator must approve an exact Work cap and contained purchasing allocation before PO approval. Approval reserves both caps under the canonical Work lock; draft preparation reserves neither. The approved reservation persists after supplier receipt and invoice/cost reconciliation, because the purchase has consumed the budget even when the outstanding financial commitment reaches zero. An approved, unsent, unposted PO has a typed cancellation that releases its reservation once and retains the decision history. Design-only windows remain separate. Historical approved POs without this authority are visibly held for an explicit transition rather than assigned synthetic approval. The controlled local test-submission action still sends nothing to Graybar. See [isolated qualification](./evidence/d5o-purchasing-economic-authority-20261017.md).
