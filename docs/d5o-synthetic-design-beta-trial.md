# D5O synthetic Design-only beta — local trial

This beta uses **fictional data only**. It is for learning the work journey and reporting usability or control defects. It does not authorize real customer work, employee time, purchasing, field execution, payment or financial reporting. Rybex may decide its production operating policies during the beta, before any real use.

Open [the local D5O preview](http://127.0.0.1:61651/work?workspace=rybex&view=my-work) on this Windows host. The displayed build should be `ba71c3a18cf7`. If it differs or signed-in Work does not load, stop and tell the local trial operator. This link works only on the host computer; remote participant access has not been published.

The local operator holds the **fictional** Admin, Project Manager, Finance, Operations Leader and second Project Manager sign-ins outside this guide. Ask the operator for only your assigned test account. Do not put passwords in feedback or screenshots. Use **Account → Switch account** to sign out before another role signs in; separate browser profiles are better when two people must decide independently. These are already confirmed fictional local accounts; signing in creates no new user and sends no email.

The five exercises form one team journey. Open [the starting Work](http://127.0.0.1:61651/work?workspace=rybex&view=my-work&record=rybex-c58496fb951241ee8e18c5ada30fd160) in your assigned role, then select its card in **My Work** and **Open exact task →**. After a decision, select **Return to refreshed My Work**; an old exact-task link may say the task changed or was decided. Do not resubmit that old link.

1. **Discover intake — Project Manager.** Prerequisite: the starting Work shows **Prepare Discover intake**. Record the fictional customer need, requester, intended outcome, rough value, date and known unknowns. Select **Save intake revision**, reload, then **Submit intake for pursuit decision**. Expected: the saved values remain after reload and an independent **Review Discover intake** appears in the Admin’s My Work. A rough value is context, not permission to spend. Tell the operator about any missing field or unclear save result for [the feedback log](./evidence/d5o-synthetic-beta-feedback-20261019.md).
2. **Independent requirements handoff — Admin, then Project Manager, Finance and Operations Leader.** Prerequisite: exercise 1 is submitted. The Admin opens **Review Discover intake** in My Work, records a fictional decision basis and selects **Qualify pursuit**; the Project Manager then submits the **Prepare Define handoff** brief, and an Admin account separate from the PM accepts **Receive Define handoff**. The PM opens **Prepare definition**, records included scope, exclusions, acceptance criteria, access/interface facts and unknowns, and requests the configured Commercial and Delivery reviews. Finance and Operations Leader use their own My Work tasks for those reviews; the PM sends the approved exact revision to Develop, where Operations Leader receives it. Expected: the PM sees Define preparation after receipt, reviewers see their own tasks, and Develop identifies the accepted Define basis. If a task is absent, stop and record the role, Work title and last visible decision instead of using another role’s account.
3. **ROM and separate Design authority — Project Manager, independent commercial reviewer and Admin.** Prerequisite: Develop has received the approved Define revision. The PM opens the Work’s **Develop** preparation, links the applicable requirements to a proposed solution, estimating quantities, sourced rates and reviewable ROM. The independent commercial reviewer handles the resulting My Work review; record a *fictional* customer budget response through the existing commercial task. Then use the separate Design funding/commitment tasks with the authorized fictional Admin reviewer. Expected: **ROM** means a rough budget estimate, not final engineering or a guaranteed price. A budget response leaves customer award unchanged; an approved Design limit is separate, and a draft Design commitment consumes nothing until independently approved. Record a blocker if the task does not identify the next reviewer or missing basis.
4. **Sourced cost and outlook — Project Manager and independent Finance reviewer.** Prerequisite: the Design commitment is independently approved. The PM opens its Design-cost task, attaches the operator-supplied **fictional** PDF through the private source control, and submits a cost claim using the supported rate/calculation basis. Finance opens the corresponding review task under their own account, reviews the exact claim, then independently reviews the Work cost outlook. Expected: **incurred cost** is reviewed cost of fictional work; **remaining commitment** is approved spend not yet realized; **additional forecast** is expected cost outside those commitments. The outlook adds those three categories once and identifies missing sources. Change a forecast through its normal form and check that the earlier assessment becomes **outdated** until independently reassessed. A PDF calculation note supports only its stated synthetic cost basis; it is not proof of real employee hours.
5. **Read the completed example and boundaries — Project Manager, Finance, Operations Leader and second Project Manager.** Open [the completed reference Work](http://127.0.0.1:61651/work?workspace=rybex&view=record&record=rybex-e2d19db1e53c46eeb951cca83cfdf92f&section=Operate&focus=finance&taskKind=full), reload, and inspect **Finance**. Expected: USD 600 reviewed incurred, USD 0 outstanding commitment and USD 250 additional forecast produce USD 850 known projected cost; an older USD 800 decision remains in history as outdated. Owner PM and Finance can retrieve the attached synthetic source; Operations and another PM receive an access denial without document bytes or a usable link. Inspect My Work and Finance at a narrow phone width if available. Do not attempt purchasing, field start, customer acceptance or Operate asset return.

For each problem, tell the local operator the exercise number, assigned role, Work title, action attempted, expected result, actual message, approximate time and displayed build. The operator records it in [the feedback log](./evidence/d5o-synthetic-beta-feedback-20261019.md), with a non-sensitive screenshot only if useful. Never include passwords, tokens, private PDF bytes or real business data. A blocked action is useful feedback; do not work around a guard. For an uncertain save, the operator checks its receipt/current state before anyone retries.

Known limits: this is an isolated, loopback-only preview, not a hosted deployment or availability commitment. Fictional rates, funding limits, delegates, reviews and PDFs are technical test inputs, not Rybex policy. Hosted object-loss recovery remains unqualified; the local matched metadata-and-byte restore passed, but ordinary API re-upload changed object identity. Live Graybar/AI, accounting, payroll, customer messaging and field execution are outside this beta. The separately preserved Data Hall C and October 26 labor qualification are not beta prerequisites.
