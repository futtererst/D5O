# Define to Develop ROM: isolated local workflow

The lifecycle remains Discover → Define → Develop → Design → Deploy → Operate. A ROM is an indicative Develop decision, not a delivery award or field authorization.

For a canonical Work Record, approve the structured Define revision and have a separate eligible receiver accept it into Develop. The approved requirements, acceptance criteria, interfaces, milestones and dependencies keep their IDs and revision. Unknown route lengths, productivity and quantities stay explicit assumptions. Develop records a solution option, its requirement links, delivery model, resources, access and risk basis; an independent configured reviewer approves the exact solution revision.

The Develop estimator saves sourced quantity and cost lines. Each line retains its unit, quantity calculation, source type/date, rate source and assumption. ROM coverage maps each approved obligation to one or more lines or a shared allowance; a line is charged once even if it supports several obligations. Customer responsibility must match a documented customer-owned source. Unresolved and excluded approved obligations block ROM review. An explicit reason is required for a zero-cost line. The database checks these conditions again when the estimate enters pricing review or is approved.

The low/base/high scenario varies the selected installation person-hours-per-unit line only. The server retains calculated scenario prices on the reviewed estimate. Under the fictional USD policy, 48 links × $80 material, 96 person-hours × $65 labor, 48 × $15 testing/reporting, and $1,000 equipment/mobilization yield $11,800 direct cost. Overhead and contingency each apply at 10% of direct cost, producing $14,160 included cost. At 25% target margin, the base indicative price is $18,880. The 1.5/2/3-hour scenarios yield $16,384/$18,880/$23,872. These rates, quantities and customer facts are fictional, and no production pricing policy is changed.

An independent reviewer approves the exact ROM estimate. A separately approved proposal records its indicative scope, assumptions, exclusions, information still needed, review date and Design validation work; recording customer submission does not send anything. A sourced fictional budget acknowledgment is separate from an independent Operations authorization of detailed Design extent and internal spend cap. Neither action sets the customer job to Won or satisfies the existing awarded-job package/field-start gate.

Design may retain an explicit quantity or method difference against the original ROM line, with a cause and source. Differences remain unpriced until current rates and the existing commercial/change process support a revised decision; they never overwrite the original estimate or authorize execution.

The current ROM scenario engine supports a published **target-margin** pricing policy. Budgetary/Firm estimates and their other pricing methods remain on their existing paths. This bounded ROM path does not issue detailed execution packages before a separate customer award. A claimed customer response is a fictional pilot record, not verified customer authority; production evidence custody and role provisioning remain separate release concerns.
