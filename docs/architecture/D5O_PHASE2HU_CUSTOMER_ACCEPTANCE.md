# Phase 2H-U customer acceptance

Package completion, turnover assembly, scoped customer acceptance, Operations package receipt, whole-Work customer acceptance and Operations whole-Work receipt are distinct decisions. The accepted package and its reviewed report/inspection/evidence are the turnover basis. An authorized internal recorder retains an externally signed PDF against the exact turnover or Work scope through the existing customer-decision evidence service. The fictional test uses synthetic PDF content and does not claim a live external customer identity or signature service. The hosted acceptance command verifies its tenant, Work, scope and revision and records the decision actor and audit receipt. The submitting worker cannot self-accept.

Whole-Work commands pass an empty package ID, preserving the existing hosted acceptance contract. The tenant-neutral adapter allows these actions only for canonical Work and authenticated customer management roles; its hosted RPC performs the final role, independence, revision, evidence and idempotency checks. After independent whole-Work receipt, the Work phase projection can complete Deploy and the Operations leader receives a server-derived **Receive accepted Deploy handoff** responsibility in My Work.

Deploy acceptance does not activate support or close financial records. Operate support and financial closeout remain separately controlled outcomes.
