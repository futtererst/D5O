The preserved 61644 preview still runs its older build and continued to show the old text. The separate isolated candidate at 61652 displayed build `62825725851b`; a PM mobile browser showed distinct “South zone controls installation” and “North zone controls installation” cards, with no horizontal overflow. **F-02 — Sign-in loop from workspace chooser.** On the old candidate, a fictional Finance sign-in reached the workspace chooser, but clicking Rybex returned to `/auth/sign-in`.
