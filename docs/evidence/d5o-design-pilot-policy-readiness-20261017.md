**Proposed activity.** One low-sensitivity, internal, supervised Design-only Work, with a named project manager preparing scope, ROM and cost sources; a separate Commercial/Operations receiver deciding the applicable handoffs and Design authority; an active administrator deciding the Work cap where configured; and an independent Finance reviewer assessing costs and outlook. A pilot owner and technical support owner must be appointed. No individual has been identified in the retained evidence as the accountable production policy approver.

**Permitted after entry approval:** record approved requirements, independent Develop/ROM reviews, budget acknowledgement, funded Design-only authorization, an independently approved internal Design commitment within approved Work and Design caps, supported Design cost claims and correction, Finance additional-cost forecast, and an as-of Complete or Reviewed with gaps outlook. A draft is not a commitment. Actual costs may be recorded honestly even if authority is exceeded; further commitments require the applicable exception decision.

**Systems of record and exclusions.** Until an owner approves reconciliation and cutover, customer contracts/awards and communications, time or effort records, general ledger/payroll, and document retention remain authoritative in their existing external processes. D5O must carry references back to them; its synthetic cost claim alone is not proof of real employee time. Exclude customer award, purchasing, subcontracting, field booking/start, physical installation, customer acceptance, Operate asset return, invoice/payment, Finance closeout, final margin and external messaging. Do not label ROM budget acknowledgement as an award or Design authorization as field authority. The October 26 labor booking and reminder remain separate.

**Entry conditions:** select a real, bounded Design-only scope and internal participants; appoint business and technical support owners; approve decisions 1–4 below; validate external source references and confidentiality treatment; complete focused projection-cache freshness/tenant checks; choose isolated environment, migration/release plan, backup/restore and issue routing; verify authenticated role separation and one representative cost source through its approved custody path. No live sensitive data or financial commitment before security, retention and funding approval. If those conditions are not met, continue fictional shadow-mode rehearsal, not live operating use.

**Measurable exit:** for one Work, an eligible PM can find and prepare each task; separate receivers/reviewers decide exact revisions; one Design commitment is consumed once; partial reviewed cost, remaining commitment and additional forecast reconcile after reload; a changed source visibly ages the prior outlook; reviewers distinguish Complete from Finance closeout; all blocked actions have a responsible role and disposition. Record decision IDs, elapsed time, source retrieval, wrong-role/stale/replay outcomes and user observations separately. Business sponsor signs off the workflow and open gaps; technical owner confirms restore and audit retrieval. A support owner records blocked work, assigns severity and next owner, pauses affected live commitments on source/security uncertainty, and tracks correction in the existing issue process; no automatic bypass or invented approval.
